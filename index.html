The vision

Built around informed decisions.

MOMENTUMBOT_AI is a trading technology development project exploring how market analysis, decision support, risk governance, and continuous strategy evaluation can work together. The public website introduces the project; it does not provide trading access.

Core technology

Four connected areas of research.

Each area is intended to support a more structured, transparent approach to automated trading research.

01 / MARKET

Market intelligence

Collecting, validating, and interpreting market information to support timely analysis.

02 / DECISIONS

Decision systems

Exploring signals, confidence scoring, and explainable trade decision workflows.

03 / RISK

Risk governance

Evaluating exposure, safeguards, and supervised controls before potential execution.

04 / LEARNING

Adaptive strategy research

Studying strategy memory, historical evaluation, and feedback-driven improvements.

Our approach

Research. Evaluate. Improve.

Research

Explore market-data sources and trading concepts with clear assumptions.

Evaluate

Assess strategies and risk controls through testing and evidence.

Improve

Use findings to refine models, safeguards, and system design.
